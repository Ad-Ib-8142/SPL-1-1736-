Waterways outputs are clipped to the provisional DNCC+DSCC polygon union.
- HOTOSM_DNCC_DSCC_waterway_lines.geojson: WGS84 line geometries.
- HOTOSM_DNCC_DSCC_UTM46N.gpkg: projected metre-based layers.
- waterway_lines_DNCC_DSCC_UTM46N_WKT.csv: C++-friendly WKT geometry column, EPSG:32646.
- water polygons are separate.
Source: HOTOSM Bangladesh OSM waterways export, snapshot 2026-10-07. Mapping completeness is not guaranteed.
